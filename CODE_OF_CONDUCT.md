# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in the
Intracept registry project a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Accepting constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
  or advances.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political
  attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to
[conduct@intracept.dev](mailto:conduct@intracept.dev). Reports will be
reviewed and investigated and will result in a response deemed necessary and
appropriate to the circumstances.
